About

I’m a junior in the Commonwealth Honors College at UMass Amherst, studying Managerial Economics (BS) and Psychology (BA) with a minor in Business. I’m most interested in financial services and risk, where analytical thinking and human behavior meet.

This past summer I worked as a Business Analyst Aide in SOX and Enterprise Risk Management at Con Edison. I built an executive-facing Power BI dashboard that gave leadership a department-level view of risk across 107+ emerging topics and trends, and wrote a VBA macro that cut manual data entry time by more than half.

At C&A Digital, I led a podcast initiative and a team evaluation of enterprise project management platforms, and presented our recommendations to leadership. I bring hands-on data skills in Excel, VBA, Power BI, SAS and R, and I’m comfortable turning analysis into clear insights for senior stakeholders.
